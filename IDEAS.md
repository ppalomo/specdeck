# Ideas

An inbox, with no commitment attached. This is not a backlog and not a plan: nothing here
is decided, and nothing here is scheduled.

One entry per idea, newest at the top, dated. When an idea matures it goes through
`/opsx:explore` and then `/opsx:propose`, and it is deleted from this file at that moment —
so the same idea is never alive in two places.
